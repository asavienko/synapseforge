# SynapseForge — Market Outreach Strategy
_Created: 2026-03-10_

---

## 🎯 Core Positioning (Memorize This)

> **"You get a fully managed AI team — without hiring anyone."**

- Pain you solve: SMB owners know AI exists, they just can't set it up, maintain it, or trust it to keep running
- You sell: expertise + deployment + ongoing care — not just software
- Why they stay: continuous improvement compounds over time; a 6-month-refined setup is way more valuable than a fresh one

---

## 👤 ICP Profile (Who You're Targeting)

**Primary:** Small business owners (5–50 employees, EU/US)
- E-commerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce)
- Local service businesses (law firms, clinics, agencies)
- SaaS founders who need AI for customer support/onboarding
- Real estate agents / brokers
- Online course creators / coaches

**What they have in common:**
- Revenue: €100k–€5M/yr
- Real, identifiable AI problem (support, lead qual, appointment booking)
- Decision maker = the owner (no procurement cycle)
- Budget: $499/mo is 1 client or 5 hours of their time — not a hard decision if value is clear

**What they don't have:**
- An in-house dev who knows AI
- Time to configure and maintain AI tools themselves
- Confidence that AI they set up won't break and embarrass them

## 📅 90-Day Plan

### Month 1: Warm Network + Beta
**Goal:** 3 beta users, 1 paying customer, real feedback

#### Week 1: List Building
- Write down 50 people you know who own or work at SMBs
- Sort by: Do they have a pain I can solve? (support volume, lead gen, repetitive work)
- Top 20 = outreach targets, rest = audience builders

#### Week 2: Personal Outreach (20 messages)
**Channel:** WhatsApp / Telegram / LinkedIn DM (wherever you have rapport)
**Tone:** Not a pitch. A question.

Template A (friends/close network):
```
Hey [name], quick one — I'm building a service where I manage AI setups for small businesses. Not software, actual ongoing management. Wondering if you know anyone running a business who's tried to use AI but it never quite worked out? I'm looking for 3 beta testers — free for 3 months.
```

Template B (acquaintances):
```
Hey [name], hope you're well. I'm launching SynapseForge — we handle AI for small business owners end-to-end. I'm currently looking for 3 companies to join as beta testers (free for 3 months, just want real feedback). Is that something that could be relevant for you or anyone you know?
```

**Goal:** 5 conversations → 3 beta users

#### Week 3: Onboard Beta Users
- Set up their instance in one session
- Document the friction points
- Check in 3 days later with a short Loom

#### Week 4: Upgrade Conversation
- Based on feedback, pitch the paid plan to the ones who got value
- Offer founder pricing: $299/mo first 3 months (instead of $499)
- Target: 1 paying customer by end of month 1

### Month 2: LinkedIn Authority + Community
**Goal:** 50 followers who care, 5 more pipeline leads

#### LinkedIn Strategy

**Content cadence:** 3 posts/week
| Type | Frequency | Examples |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Story posts | 1/week | "A client came to us with X problem. Here's what we built." |
| Educational | 1/week | "5 AI automations every e-commerce store should have" |
| Behind the scenes | 1/week | "What managing AI for 3 businesses actually looks like" |

**Post formula (Story post):**
```
[Hook — the problem]
A restaurant owner was spending 4 hours a week answering the same 20 questions on Instagram.

[What happened]
We set up a WhatsApp AI for them. Trained it on their menu, hours, booking process.

[The result]
3 weeks in: 80% of inquiries handled without human intervention. Owner now spends those 4 hours on actual work.

[The offer/CTA]
If you run a local business and you're drowning in repetitive messages, this is exactly what we do at SynapseForge.

Drop a comment or DM me — happy to show you what it would look like for your business.
```

**LinkedIn DM Outreach (20/week, warm leads only):**
Target: people who engage with your posts, or 2nd-degree connections in SMB space
```
Hey [name], saw you run [business type] — we work a lot with [same space] on AI support/automation. Not pitching anything, just noticed the overlap. What's the biggest time sink in your operations right now?
```
(Listen first. Pitch only if they have the problem.)

#### Community Strategy
Join and participate (don't spam):

**Slack communities:**
- Indie Hackers (indiehackers.com/community)
- Online Geniuses (marketers/SMB owners)
- SaaStr Community
- Entrepreneurs On Fire (EO community)

**Reddit:**
- r/entrepreneur — post stories and case studies
- r/smallbusiness — answer questions (become the AI guy)
- r/agency — lots of service business owners

**Approach:** Answer 5 questions/week in these communities. Never pitch cold. Mention SynapseForge only when directly relevant.

### Month 3: Paid Acquisition Test + Referral Activation
**Goal:** $2k+ MRR, referral loop active

#### Referral Program Launch
- Email all existing users about the referral program
- Make it tangible: "For every friend you refer who becomes a Pro client, you get $50/mo credit. They get their first month free."
- Create a unique referral link per client in the dashboard

#### LinkedIn Ads (Small test, $200–500)
- Target: business owners, 25–55, EU/US, specific job titles (Founder, Owner, CEO — company size 1–50)
- Ad format: short video (even selfie-style) explaining the service
- Landing page: specific to the ad ("AI for [Industry] — fully managed")
- Budget: $10–20/day for 2 weeks. Kill if CAC > $300.

#### Cold Email (50 targeted prospects)
Only do this once you have 2–3 real client results to reference.

**Target list:** Find via Apollo.io or LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- E-commerce founders with Shopify stores
- Local service businesses with visible online presence (high Google review volume)
- Coaches/consultants with an audience

**Template:**
```
Subject: AI for [Company Name]

Hey [Name],

Found [Company] while researching [industry] businesses in [region].

Quick question: do you have anyone handling your customer messages / lead follow-up, or is that still you?

We run AI for SMBs — fully managed, ongoing. One of our clients is [similar business type] — we automated X, saved them Y hours/week.

Worth a 15-min call to see if it applies to you?

— Anton
SynapseForge
```

## 💬 Objection Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We tried chatbots, they sucked" | "I get it — most do. The difference is we maintain it. A bot trained once dies. Ours gets better every week." |
| "We don't have budget right now" | "Starter is free. I'll set it up, you use it, you decide if it's worth paying for." |
| "I don't understand AI" | "Good — you don't have to. That's literally what we're for." |
| "What if it breaks?" | "We monitor every instance. Health checks every 5 minutes. Rollback if anything goes wrong. You have a manager, not just software." |
| "How do I know it'll work for us?" | "It won't work for everyone. Let me ask you a few questions first — if I don't think we can help, I'll tell you." |
